# Home

The shared brain of you and Claude Code. Plain Markdown: you read and edit it in Obsidian, Claude reads and
writes it every session. Open this folder as an Obsidian vault and use the Graph view to see it connected.

## How it learns (ACE: reflect, then curate one item at a time)
1. Every answer adds one line to the **Diary** (a hook writes it; nobody has to remember).
2. Every request brings back the 3 most related lessons (**recall** hook), so a lesson shows up when it matters.
3. Your corrections and confirmations are detected automatically → [[Corrections]].
4. Once a day the review distills the diary into [[Lessons]], each with date and evidence, and bumps the
   «helped / failed» counter of the lessons that were used. On Mondays, lessons older than 14 days are re-checked:
   «is this still true today?».
5. Five days in a row at zero on a front raises an [[Alerts|alert]]: change course, do not polish.

## Notes
- [[Lessons]]: strategy lessons, read first. One page at most.
- [[Technical lessons]]: what broke and how not to repeat it.
- [[Working with me]]: how the human wants to work, in their own words, with dates.
- [[Direction]]: the goal and what was decided.
- [[Experiments]]: every test with hypothesis, measure, date and result. New ideas need a demand signal.
- [[Alerts]] and [[Corrections]]: written by the hooks.
